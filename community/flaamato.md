### 🎯 Professional Overview
**Art Director, Senior Graphic Designer, and Motion Designer** with over **10 years of experience** leading creative strategy, brand development, audiovisual production, and $360^\circ$ campaigns. 

I have delivered high-impact creative direction and multi-channel visual assets for multinational brands including **Ubisoft, GOL Linhas Aéreas, Scania, Mars, Copersucar, and Pedigree**, as well as leading brand and broadcast design within major esports circuits (LATAM Esports Leagues, SWS Gaming, Six Invitational). 

Currently expanding my technical depth into **Software Engineering**, bridging the gap between design leadership, user research (UX), aesthetic rigor, and scalable code.

- 🎓 **Education:** B.A. in Visual Communication (UAM/ESPM) | MBA in UX Research, ResearchOps & Design Leadership | MBA in Audiovisual Design (UAM) | MBA in Design in Motion (UAM).
- 💡 **Core Expertise:** Creative Direction, Brand Systems & Guidelines, Kinetic Typography, Fast Turnaround Audiovisual Production, UX/UI Design, Generative AI Workflows (Midjourney, Firefly).
- ⚙️ **Technical Focus:** Software Architecture, Git/GitHub, Frontend Development, JavaScript, HTML5/CSS3.
